02 / CONSTRUCTION

The pause belongs to the path.

GRID
4 × 4 equally spaced points.
x, y = 3, 9, 15, 21 on a 24 px canvas.
STROKE
1.5 px, square caps, mitred joins.
One underlying route; only straight lines or quarter arcs.
GAP
One grid unit of visible clearance along the route.
Cut lengths compensate for square caps.
DOT
At most one 2 × 2 px solid square, centred on a grid point. It alone may take Signal Blue.
TYPE
D-DIN Condensed Bold, capitals, 0.06em tracking.
IBM Plex Mono for labels, tags and “Zyn prototype”.

A route with a review gap is continuous in construction and interrupted in ink. Gap dimensions refer to visible clearance along the route. Cuts compensate for the 0.75 px square-cap projection. Route vertices stay on-grid; paint cuts can sit between points, including the approved diagonal cut in Z.

03 / IN-APP ICONS

Judgements have an author.

Code check uses angle brackets. The AI reviewer uses an open eye. A person uses a head and shoulders. Keep their names beside the icons in the app.

44 icons

Approve / Passed and Reject / Failed share their respective shapes. Running is recognisable without animation. Status remains labelled and works without colour.

04 / DELIVERY

Ready to inspect. Ready to use.

Portable SVGs use currentColor for ink. Wordmarks are outlined. The archive includes themed marks and wordmarks at 512, 64, 32 and 16 px, plus all 44 icons at 24 and 16 px.
