# Zyn wiki

**Traceable work. Checked twice. Decided by a person.**

Zyn is a prototype checker and benchmark for automotive engineering work: requirements, tests and CAN databases. A model may propose; code checks the proposal; an independent AI reviewer gives advice; a named person decides. Every step is written to an append-only audit log.

Zyn does not try to out-generate the many tools that write requirements and tests. It checks their output, including output from tools other than Zyn, and it measures the models behind them on public answer keys.

## Ground rules

- **Prototype.** Every screen and export says "Zyn prototype." Nothing is verified until a named reviewer approves it.
- **Public and synthetic data only.** No company or customer documents.
- **No productivity claims.** Zyn reports what it measured on named datasets, with the method beside the number.
- **Honest answer keys.** The synthetic ICX-100 specification and its answer key were written by Claude and checked by an AI reviewer, not by an engineer. Every score on it says so.
